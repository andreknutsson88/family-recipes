#### Directions
- Cut the green onions in half separating the green and white parts.  Split the white parts in half and cut them in 2 inc sections. cut the green pasts in 2 inch sections as well and keep separate for later
- Mince garlic and cut onion in large strips
- Cut beef into preferred size
- Marinate the beef with soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, baking soda and corn starch and mix well.
- Add oil and sesame oil to  the beef marinade.
- heat a pan with oil. When hot add onion and white part of green onion. Stir fry just to remove raw and spicy taste, then season with sugar and salt. Do not over cook, remove from pan when ready 
- Add more oil to the pan and and the beef. fry without stirring until bottom is a bit brown. start stirring and add garlic. Continue to stirr until 70% done.
- Add rice wine around the edge of the pan and let cook off
- Add dark soy sauce and oyster sauce.
- Add water.
- When sauce starts to thicken add black pepper onion and green onion and stirr
- You are done!
