### Ingredients
#ingredients 
- [x] 800g Raw [[Kycklingbröst]] cut into cubes  
- [x] 1.5 Tsp [[Salt]]  
- [x] 1 Tsp [[Svartpeppar]]  
- [x] 1 Tsp [[Chilli Flakes]] (leave it out if you don’t want it spicy)  
- [x] 1.5 Tsp [[Vitlökspulver]]  
- [x] 1.5 Tsp [[Lökpulver]]  
- [x] 1.5 Tep [[Paprikapulver]]  
- [x] 2 Tsp [[Olivolja]]  
- [x] 30g Light [[Smör]] (Brand: Lurpak) for cooking + extra once cooked  
- [x] 2 Tsp Minced [[Vitlök]]  
- [x] 30g [[Honung]] (15g Honey per batch of chicken)  
- [x] Handful Fresh Chopped [[Persilja]]


### Directions

- Cut chicken in bite size cubes
- Add salt, black pepper, chili flakes and garlic powder onion powder and olive oil
- Get a pan on medium heat first then add butter. Let it melt then add your chicken (don’t overcrowd the pan). Cook for 4-5 mins on one side first till you get a nice golden brown crust. Then cook the other side for 3-4 mins more.  
  
Lower the heat completely when adding the extra butter, garlic and honey. Mix till well coated and sticky then set aside
